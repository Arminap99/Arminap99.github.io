Need help?

Write to questagames@outlook.com and tell us your device model, Android/iOS version and what happened. Screenshots help!

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language?

Open the pause menu (☰) and tap the 🌐 language button. The game restarts in the language you pick.

The game feels slow on my phone

Open the pause menu and set “Graphics quality” to Low. The game also lowers the quality by itself if it detects a slow device.

Can I move my progress to another phone?

Progress is saved on the device only, so it cannot be moved or restored after uninstalling.

How do I get gold coins?

Complete missions, catch thieves, chase away rival vendors, please the health inspector, collect the daily reward and finish restaurants.

I bought coins but did not get them

Restart the game: purchases that did not finish are credited when it starts. If the coins are still missing, write to us with your order number (from the receipt e-mail of Google Play or Apple).

How do I stop seeing ads?

Ads are optional: they appear only if you tap “Watch”. In the EU/UK you can change your consent under “Ad privacy options” in the pause menu.
