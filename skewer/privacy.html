Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Summary

Kooche (formerly Skewer by Skewer) (“the game”) has no accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any other personal information. We run no servers and no analytics of our own. Your progress is saved only on your device.

The versions of the game on Google Play and the Apple App Store offer optional rewarded ads (served by Google AdMob) and in-app purchases of gold coins. This page explains what those two services process. The versions for the Iranian stores (Cafe Bazaar, Myket) contain no third-party advertising or analytics service.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (language, sound, graphics quality, the date of the last daily reward, and the identifiers of coin purchases that were already credited) are saved only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are removed when you uninstall the game or choose “Erase save and start over” in the game menu.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one (for free coins or a boost). To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep ads relevant, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process: your device’s advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information (model, OS version, app name and version, language), and how you interact with the ad. This data is processed by Google under Google’s advertising policies and Privacy Policy; we do not receive it and we do not combine it with anything else.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time with “Ad privacy options” in the game menu. On iPhone the game asks for App Tracking Transparency permission; you can say no and still play everything. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The store tells the game only that a purchase succeeded (product and a purchase token), which the game uses to credit the coins once. Gold coins have no cash value.

What we do not collect

  • Name, e-mail address or any account data
  • Location data (other than the approximate location Google may derive from the IP address for ads, as described above), contacts, photos, camera or microphone data
  • Our own usage statistics or crash reports

Internet use and links

Playing needs no internet. It is used only for ads and purchases. A few buttons (for example the link to our other game, Questa, on the web, Telegram or Instagram) open your browser or another app; those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Data retention and your rights

We hold no personal data about you. Data processed by Google for ads is governed by Google’s policies and the controls linked above. If you have a question about your data, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy we will update this page and the store listings before the update is released.
