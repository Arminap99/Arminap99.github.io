What is inside

Grill, carry, serve

Load the skewers, flip them just in time and serve your hungry customers. Keep the queue happy and never let anything burn!

9 different restaurants

Kebab, liver grill, burger, pizza, grilled fish, fast food, kalle-pache, pasta and a traditional kebab house, each with its own menu and cooking station.

Hire & train a pro team

Helpers, cooks, waiters, dishwashers, cashiers and drive-thru runners. Train them from rookie to pro, with outfits to match.

Coffee, chess & a smart accountant

Open a tea and coffee bar with its own barista, put chess and backgammon on the tables, install a projector, and let your accountant collect the cash and pay the wages.

Expect the unexpected

City officers, health inspectors, food bloggers, a cheeky cat, a thief, a rival vendor and the lunch rush will keep you busy.

Easy one-thumb play

Drag to move, stand on the glowing tiles to upgrade. Works offline: the internet is only needed for ads and purchases.

9 languages

English, فارسی, العربية, Türkçe, Русский, Español, Português, Français and Deutsch.
