Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag your finger anywhere on the screen to walk. Stand in the coloured area next to a chair, basin or table to work on the client there. Stand on a yellow square to buy an upgrade or hire staff. “How to play” in the pause menu (☰) explains everything.

Is the game in English?

Not yet: the game is in Persian (Farsi) for now.

The game feels slow on my phone

Open the pause menu and set “Graphics quality” to Low. The game also lowers the quality by itself if it detects a slow device.

Can I move my progress to another phone?

Progress is saved on the device only, so it cannot be moved or restored after uninstalling.
