Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Summary

Hair Salon Tycoon: Kooche (“the game”) has no accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any other personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking service, and we run no servers. Your progress is saved only on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (the name you give your salon, sound, graphics quality and the date of the last daily reward) are saved only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They are removed when you uninstall the game or choose “Erase save and start over” in the game menu.

What we do not collect

  • Name, e-mail address or any account data
  • Location
  • Contacts, photos or files
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage or crash data

Internet use and links

Playing needs no internet. A few buttons (for example the link to our other game, Questa, on the web, Telegram or Instagram) open in your browser or in that app; what happens there is covered by those sites’ own policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a later version adds advertising or in-app purchases, or changes the game in any other way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the store listing before that version is released.
