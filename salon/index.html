What is inside

Cut, wash, colour

Stand next to a client to get to work. Hair really gets shorter, clippings fall on the floor, and colour changes it for good.

By hand, then staff, then machines

Every new department starts with you. Then you hire someone, then a machine takes over, and your hands move on.

Five stages

A one-chair barbershop, a three-chair salon, a colour bar, nails and spa, and a celebrity studio with a wall of fame.

The picture

Some clients bring a photo of the style they want. Take it on for nearly double, or offer a simple cut when you are busy.

Brides and stars

Get a bridal party ready before the clock runs out. Send a celebrity off happy and the whole city wants your salon.

Keep them happy

Mind the dryer hood, sweep the floor, pay the bills and your staff, and handle complaints at the desk.
